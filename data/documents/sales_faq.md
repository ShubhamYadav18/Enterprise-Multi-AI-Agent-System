# Sales FAQ — Acme Corporation

## Pricing

### Q: What are the pricing tiers for Acme Enterprise Suite?

| Plan | Users | Price/Year | Includes |
|------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Starter** | Up to 50 | $25,000 | Workflow Engine + Basic Support |
| **Professional** | Up to 200 | $75,000 | All Products + Priority Support |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited | $150,000+ | All Products + Dedicated CSM + Custom SLA |

### Q: Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. Organizations with 500+ users receive a **15% discount**. Multi-year contracts (3+ years) qualify for an additional **10% discount**.

### Q: Is there a free trial?
We offer a **14-day free trial** of the Professional plan. No credit card required. Contact sales@acmecorp.example.com to get started.

## Sales Process

### Q: What is the typical sales cycle?
- **Starter**: 2–4 weeks (self-service available).
- **Professional**: 4–8 weeks with solution architect engagement.
- **Enterprise**: 8–16 weeks with custom scoping and executive alignment.

### Q: Who handles enterprise deals?
Enterprise deals are managed by our **Strategic Accounts** team. Each enterprise client is assigned a dedicated Account Executive and Solution Architect.

## CRM and Tools

### Q: What CRM do we use?
We use **Salesforce** as our primary CRM. All leads, opportunities, and customer interactions must be logged in Salesforce within **24 hours**.

### Q: How are leads assigned?
Leads are assigned via **round-robin** within regional teams. Inbound leads from the website are routed to the **SDR team** for initial qualification.

## Commission Structure

- **Starter deals**: 8% commission on first-year revenue.
- **Professional deals**: 10% commission on first-year revenue.
- **Enterprise deals**: 12% commission on first-year revenue.
- **Renewals**: 4% commission on renewal revenue.
- Accelerators apply at **120% quota attainment** (1.5x multiplier).
