# Product Overview — Acme Corporation

## Acme Enterprise Suite

Acme Enterprise Suite is our flagship platform, providing integrated business operations management for mid-to-large enterprises.

### Core Products

#### 1. Acme Workflow Engine
An intelligent process automation platform that streamlines business operations.
- **Visual Workflow Designer**: Drag-and-drop interface for building complex workflows.
- **AI-Powered Routing**: Automatic task assignment based on workload and expertise.
- **Integration Hub**: Pre-built connectors for 200+ enterprise systems.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time process metrics and bottleneck identification.
- **Pricing**: Starting at $15,000/year for up to 100 users.

#### 2. Acme DataVault
A secure enterprise data management solution.
- **Unified Data Lake**: Centralize data from multiple sources.
- **Role-Based Access**: Granular permissions with audit trails.
- **Compliance Engine**: Automated GDPR, SOC 2, and HIPAA compliance checks.
- **Pricing**: Starting at $25,000/year based on data volume.

#### 3. Acme Insights
A business intelligence and analytics platform.
- **Natural Language Queries**: Ask questions in plain English.
- **Predictive Analytics**: ML-powered forecasting and anomaly detection.
- **Custom Dashboards**: Build and share interactive reports.
- **Pricing**: Starting at $10,000/year for up to 50 users.
