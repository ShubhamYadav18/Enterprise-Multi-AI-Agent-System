# Leave Policy — Acme Corporation

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month.

### Carryover
- Unused leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of **5 days**.
- Carried-over leave must be used by March 31 of the following year.

### Requesting Leave
1. Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least **5 business days** in advance.
2. Requests exceeding 5 consecutive days require **manager approval** and a **2-week notice**.
3. Leave during peak periods (December 15 – January 5) is subject to team availability.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **12 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry over.

- For absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**, a medical certificate is required.
- Sick leave may be used for personal medical appointments with manager notification.

## Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
- **Secondary caregiver**: 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Parental leave may be taken within the first **12 months** of the child's birth or adoption.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to **5 days** of bereavement leave for immediate family members and **2 days** for extended family members.

## Public Holidays

All employees observe **10 company holidays** as listed in the Company Holidays document. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest working day will be designated as the observed holiday.
