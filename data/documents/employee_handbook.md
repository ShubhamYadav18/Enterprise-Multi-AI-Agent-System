# Employee Handbook — Acme Corporation

## Our Mission

Acme Corporation is dedicated to delivering innovative enterprise solutions that empower organizations to operate more efficiently. Founded in 2010, we have grown to over 2,500 employees across 12 global offices.

## Core Values

1. **Innovation** — We encourage creative problem-solving and continuous improvement.
2. **Integrity** — We conduct business honestly and transparently.
3. **Collaboration** — We believe the best results come from working together.
4. **Excellence** — We hold ourselves to the highest standards.
5. **Inclusion** — We build a diverse workplace where everyone belongs.

## Code of Conduct

- Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Protect company intellectual property and confidential information.
- Report any conflicts of interest to your manager or HR.
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.
- Use company resources responsibly and for authorized business purposes.

## Workplace Policies

### Working Hours
Standard working hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM**, Monday through Friday. Flexible working arrangements are available with manager approval.

### Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with prior arrangement. Full-time remote work requires VP-level approval and a signed remote work agreement.

### Dress Code
Business casual is the standard dress code. Client-facing meetings may require business formal attire.

### Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted **bi-annually** (June and December). Reviews include self-assessment, manager evaluation, and goal-setting for the next period.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who observe violations of company policy may report concerns confidentially through the **Ethics Hotline** at ethics@acmecorp.example.com or through the anonymous reporting portal.
