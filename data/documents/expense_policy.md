# Expense Policy — Acme Corporation

## General Guidelines

All business expenses must be **pre-approved** by your direct manager for amounts exceeding $100. Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date.

## Travel Expenses

### Flights
- **Domestic**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval.
- **International**: Business class is permitted for flights over 6 hours.
- Book through the company travel portal (Concur) at least **14 days** in advance for best rates.

### Hotels
- Standard corporate rate: up to **$200/night** for domestic travel.
- International travel: up to **$300/night** or local market rate, whichever is lower.
- Extended stays (5+ nights) should use corporate housing arrangements.

### Ground Transportation
- Ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) is preferred over rental cars for single-day travel.
- Rental cars are approved for multi-day trips or locations with limited transit.
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicles: **$0.67/mile** (2025 IRS rate).

## Meals and Entertainment

### Per Diem Rates
| Meal | Domestic | International |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Breakfast | $20 | $25 |
| Lunch | $30 | $40 |
| Dinner | $50 | $65 |

### Client Entertainment
- Client dinners: up to **$150/person** with prior manager approval.
- Team events: budget of **$75/person/quarter** managed by team leads.

## Reimbursement Process

1. Collect all receipts (digital photos accepted).
2. Submit expense report via **Concur** with itemized receipts.
3. Manager approval within **5 business days**.
4. Reimbursement processed in the next payroll cycle (typically 10–15 business days).

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal entertainment or mini-bar charges
- Traffic violations or parking tickets
- Airline upgrades without prior approval
- Expenses without receipts exceeding $25
