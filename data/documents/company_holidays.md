## Holiday Policies

### Weekend Holidays
When a holiday falls on a **Saturday**, the preceding Friday is observed. When it falls on a **Sunday**, the following Monday is observed.

### Floating Holidays
In addition to the 10 company holidays, employees receive **2 floating holidays** per year that may be used for religious observances, cultural celebrations, or personal occasions. Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year and do not carry over.

### Holiday Work
Employees required to work on company holidays receive **1.5x their standard hourly rate** (or equivalent comp time for salaried employees). Holiday work must be pre-approved by a department head.

### Office Closures
The office is **fully closed** from December 24 through January 1 (Winter Break). Employees are not required to use PTO for this period — it is a company-paid shutdown.

## International Offices

International offices observe local public holidays as defined by the regional HR team. The total number of holidays is standardized to **10 days** per region to ensure parity.
